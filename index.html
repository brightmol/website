About Us

Bridging computational design and biological reality

BrightMol Biotech is an academic spin-off from the University of Trento, founded in 2025 to accelerate early-stage drug discovery. We bridge the gap between computational drug design and biological validation through our proprietary LIPS technology, enabling real-time monitoring of target proteins inside living cells.

By providing faster, more informative, and cost-effective cell-based assays, we help pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies identify promising drug candidates earlier, reduce development risk, and make better R&D decisions. Our long-term vision is to transform biological validation into a faster, smarter, and more predictive process - accelerating the development of new therapies for patients worldwide.

Our Technology

LIPS: Light Identification of Protein Suppressors

LIPS is a novel phenotypic screening platform that rapidly identifies bioactive molecules capable of modulating the stability and expression levels of specific target proteins, directly within their native intracellular environment.

By shifting from static endpoint measurements to real-time monitoring and multiparametric readouts, LIPS lets pharma R&D teams make earlier, more robust, and highly informed go/no-go decisions.

What We Do

Service packages for the pharmaceutical industry

Four project types, each designed to plug into a different stage of your discovery pipeline - from first screen to lead optimization.

01

Hit Identification & Target Screening

Accelerated discovery and identification of novel bioactive molecules

  • High Content Screening (HCS): automated multiparametric screening for synthetic compound libraries and complex natural product extracts
  • CRISPR screening / genetic modulators to decipher target interactions with protein stability pathways
  • Customized screening campaigns designed around your specific target of interest
02

Drug Candidate Validation & Custom Assays

Functional validation and high-fidelity biological contextualization of your leads

  • Custom cell-based assays: in-house engineering of cell lines tailored to monitor real-time protein degradation or stabilization
  • High-fidelity validation of drug candidates in advanced cell-based assays
  • Dose-response activity evaluation to calculate the EC₅₀ of active compounds
03

MoA Studies & Hit-to-Lead Optimization

Deep pharmacodynamic insights and molecular refinement

  • Characterization of cell signaling pathways and interaction kinetics with the target protein
  • Hit-to-lead optimization: iterative biological and analytical support to develop analogs with improved potency and selectivity
04

Early Preliminary Toxicity Assays

Timely pipeline de-risking to prevent late-stage developmental failures

  • Preliminary in vitro cytotoxicity assessment across healthy human cell lines
  • Monitoring of compound-induced phenotypic alterations and cellular stress via High Content Imaging
  • Early toxicity metrics for preliminary LD₅₀ calculation in cell models
